ABOUT THE CONFERENCE

The Department of Mathematics, NSUT, New Delhi, invites researchers, academicians, scientists, and professionals from across the globe to submit original research papers for RAM&A-2026. The conference aims to provide an international platform for discussing recent advances in both pure and applied mathematics, with special emphasis on their applications to real-world problems.

RAM&A-2026 seeks to promote intellectual exchange, interdisciplinary collaboration, and academic excellence by bringing together experts from diverse fields of mathematical sciences.

ABOUT THE UNIVERSITY

Netaji Subhas University of Technology (NSUT), New Delhi, is one of India's premier technical universities, renowned for excellence in engineering, applied sciences, and interdisciplinary research. With a strong tradition of academic excellence and innovation, NSUT provides a dynamic environment for learning, research, and industry collaboration. The University is committed to nurturing skilled professionals, researchers, and leaders who contribute to technological advancement and societal development.

EXPLORE THE SCOPE

Submission Topics

Topics of interest for submission include, but are not limited to:

01 Pure Mathematics
Algebra, Analysis, Functional Analysis, Complex Analysis, Operator Theory, Approximation Theory, and related areas.
02Applied & Computational Mathematics
Scientific Computing, Differential Equations, Mathematical Modelling, Optimization, Data-Driven Computation, and Computational Mathematics.
03Probability, Statistics & Mathematical Sciences
Probability, Statistics, Stochastic Modelling, Financial Mathematics, and related disciplines.
04Cosmology & Astrophysics
Gravitation, Cosmology, Astrophysics, Black Holes, Gravitational Lensing, Radiating Stars, Dark Matter, Dark Energy.
05Information Security & Emerging Technologies
Cryptography, Coding Theory, Cybersecurity, Fuzzy Computing and Uncertainty Quantification, Mobile Computing, Big Data Analytics, and related mathematical foundations.
06Artificial Intelligence & Interdisciplinary Applications
Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Data Science, Scientific Computing, Intelligent Systems, Mathematical Applications in AI.
