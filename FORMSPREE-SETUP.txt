FORMSPREE SETUP — RAM&A 2026

The Contact > Submit Your Query form is now configured for Formspree.

1. Create a free Formspree account at https://formspree.io/
2. Create a new form and copy its Form ID.
3. In contact.html, replace YOUR_FORM_ID in:
   https://formspree.io/f/YOUR_FORM_ID
   with your actual Formspree Form ID.
4. Upload the updated contact.html to your repository.

The form will collect:
- Name
- Email ID
- Subject
- Message

Submissions will appear in your Formspree dashboard. The visitor remains on the website and receives a confirmation state after successful submission.
