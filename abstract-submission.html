Submit Your Abstract

Follow the two simple steps below to prepare your abstract and complete your submission for the conference.

1

Download the abstract template

Please prepare your abstract using either the Word or LaTeX template provided below.

Download Word Template (.docx) → Download LaTeX Template (.tex) →
2

Submit your abstract

Once your abstract is ready, submit the completed file through the official Microsoft CMT submission portal.

Submit Abstract via Microsoft CMT →
Abstract submission deadline
October 31, 2026

3

Submit your full paper

Accepted contributors can submit their full paper through the same Microsoft CMT submission portal.

Submit Full Paper via Microsoft CMT →
