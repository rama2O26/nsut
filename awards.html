Awards & Recognition

RAM&A 2026 recognises outstanding contributions from early-career and student researchers through awards for exceptional papers and posters.

✦
02 AWARDS
Oral Presentations

Best Paper Awards

Two awards will be presented to outstanding oral presentations, selected by a panel of experts for originality, rigour, and clarity of exposition.

  • Certificate of recognition✓
  • Cash prize₹10,000 each
✧
02 AWARDS
Poster Presentations

Best Poster Awards

Two awards will recognise exceptional poster presentations, selected for scientific merit, clarity, and relevance to the conference themes.

  • Certificate of recognition✓
  • Cash prize₹5,000 each
Selection: Awardees will be selected by a panel of experts based on the quality, originality, scientific merit, and clarity of their presentations.
