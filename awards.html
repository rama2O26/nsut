Who can win

Eligibility Criteria

The Best Paper and Best Poster awards are open to participants who meet the criteria below.

Eligibility

  • Full-length, unpublished and original research work must be submitted to be considered for the Best Paper award.
  • The participant must bring a hard or soft copy of the final manuscript.
  • The decision will be made on the basis of the originality/novelty of the research work and presentation/documentation skills.
  • The presenter must be the first author and must present in person at the conference.
  • Each person can win at most one award and can enter only one category (oral or poster) for award consideration.
Poster presentations

Poster Guidelines

Please prepare your poster to the specifications below. A ready-to-use template in the conference colours is available for download.

Poster specifications

  • SizeA0 (841 × 1189 mm)
  • OrientationPortrait
  • LanguageEnglish
  • Title60 pt or larger
  • Headings40 pt or larger
  • Body text28 pt or larger
  • Images300 dpi or higher
Download poster template PowerPoint (.pptx) · A0 portrait

Content and display

  • Include the title, all authors with affiliations, and the presenter’s email address.
  • Suggested sections: abstract, introduction and objectives, methodology, results and discussion, conclusions, references and acknowledgements.
  • Use figures, tables and diagrams in preference to long paragraphs, and keep text concise.
  • Bring a printed copy of your poster. Posters are displayed during the scheduled poster session.
  • The presenting author must stand by the poster during the session, and during judging, to answer questions.
  • Keep the conference logos and footer in the template unchanged.
Oral presentations

Oral Presentation Guidelines

Please follow these guidelines so that every session runs smoothly and on time.

Preparing your talk

  • Presentations are delivered in English.
  • Each talk is given a fixed time slot, including time for questions. The duration will be announced in the final programme.
  • Prepare slides in PowerPoint (.pptx) or PDF format. Start with a title slide showing the title, authors and affiliations.
  • Keep slides clear and uncluttered, with large, legible text, and present the main result and its significance.
  • Rehearse your talk so that you finish within the allotted time.

On the day

  • Bring your slides on a USB drive and upload them to the session computer before your session begins.
  • Report to the session chair at least 10 minutes before your session starts.
  • Session chairs will keep to the schedule strictly and may stop a talk that overruns.
  • Award judges will attend the oral sessions. Only in-person presentations are considered for the Best Paper Awards.
Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I compete if I am presenting online?

No. Although the conference runs in hybrid mode, only presenters who present in person are considered for the Best Paper and Best Poster awards.

Can I enter both the oral and poster categories?

No. Each person may enter one category only and can receive at most one award.

Who counts as an early-career researcher?

Researchers within 5 years of being awarded their PhD. Master’s and PhD students are also eligible.

Do I need to be registered to be considered?

Yes. The presenter must be a registered participant and must have paid the registration fee.

Can a co-author receive the prize?

The cash prize goes to the presenting author, who must be the first author. Co-authors receive certificates of recognition.

Is it compulsory to use the poster template?

The template is recommended but not compulsory. Whatever design you use, the poster must be A0 portrait and meet the minimum font sizes listed above.

When will the winners be announced?

Winners are announced and awards presented at the closing session. Winners must be present to receive their award.

Who do I contact with questions?

Write to us at rama2026@nsut.ac.in.
